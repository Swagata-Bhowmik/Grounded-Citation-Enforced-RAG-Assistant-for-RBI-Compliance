# Regulatory Corpus — Real RBI Master Directions

This folder holds the **real, unmodified RBI Master Direction PDFs** that the entire
system is built on. Downloaded directly from the official source (rbi.org.in).

- Do **not** rename the files in a way that loses their meaning — keep them descriptive.
- These are the ground truth. Every answer, citation, and evaluation traces back here.
- A manifest (`corpus_manifest.csv`) will record each document's title, RBI reference
  number, publish/update date, source URL, and download date for full traceability.
